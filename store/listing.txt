DESCRIPTION
webTopdf by Mulham Kassab — https://mulhamkassab.dev/

Save the webpage open in Chrome as a PDF on your device.

Visual PDF captures the full loaded page or current view and downloads a paginated, image-based PDF. Choose A4 or Letter, portrait or landscape, margins, image quality, and an optional source/date footer. Hide selected page elements, undo changes, and save your preferred settings. Alt+Shift+P offers quick save with your last settings when that shortcut is available.

Browser print opens Chrome's print preview. Choose Save as PDF to create a text-friendly PDF using the website's print layout. Visual captures preserve appearance as images and do not contain selectable text; print output depends on the website.

Processing happens locally. There is no account, page-upload endpoint, remote code, advertising, or analytics. Temporary access to the current tab is granted when you invoke the extension.

Chrome restricts extension access to its internal and protected pages. Keep the captured tab active until saving finishes. Load the content you need before capture. Very long, infinite-scrolling, virtualized, embedded, or changing layouts may work better with current-view capture or browser print.

TEST INSTRUCTIONS
No account, credentials, payment, or external backend is required. Open an ordinary webpage, click webTopdf, choose Current view and Save as PDF, and confirm that Chrome downloads a PDF. Choose Full page to test scrolling capture, or Searchable print to open the browser's print preview. Use Pick & hide to temporarily remove a chosen page element, then Undo or Reset to restore it. Captures, cancellation, and recoverable failures restore page state.
