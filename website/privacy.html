YOUR PAGES STAY YOURS.

Your pages stay
on your device.

webTopdf turns webpages into PDFs on your device. It does not send page content, screenshots, or exported files to a webTopdf server.

Effective date: November 26, 2026 · Applies to the webTopdf extension and companion website.

The short version: no account, no capture upload, no analytics in the extension, and no advertising. The website only counts page visits, without cookies. You choose what to capture and where to save the resulting PDF.

Who is responsible

webTopdf is published and maintained by Mulham Kassab, an individual developer. In privacy terms, Mulham Kassab is the person responsible for the extension and this website. Because webTopdf does not collect your data on any server, the developer does not hold personal data about you.

Contact

To ask a question or make a private request, write to the developer email shown on the webTopdf Chrome Web Store listing. For non-private reports, bugs and ideas, you can also use the webTopdf issue tracker on GitHub. Please do not put private page content in public issues.

Data the extension handles

The Chrome Web Store asks developers to declare the kinds of data an extension handles. This table uses the same categories. Everything in it is used on your device only, to make the PDF you asked for. None of it is sent to the developer or to any third party, and none of it is used for advertising, profiling or analytics.

The extension also stores your chosen export settings (such as paper size and image quality) on your device using Chrome’s extension storage, and keeps the latest export status briefly for progress and download recovery. It is not a browsing-history log. The PDF may contain the page’s title, URL and visible content, including personal information shown on that page. You control that file and any sharing you do afterwards.

How it works

When you start a capture, webTopdf temporarily reads information from the active tab, such as its title, URL, dimensions and rendered appearance. A full-page export may scroll that page while taking screenshots. When you use Pick & hide, the extension handles pointer movement, clicks and the Escape key to identify and hide the elements you choose. webTopdf combines the screenshots into a PDF locally and asks Chrome to download it.

The Report a problem link in the popup only opens a GitHub page in your browser with the extension version, your browser name and the error message filled in. It never includes page content, and nothing is sent until you choose to submit the report on GitHub.

Permissions, with a purpose

The extension uses temporary access to your active tab when you interact with it. Scripting is used to inspect page dimensions, prepare the layout, and scroll during capture. Chrome’s download permission lets webTopdf save the generated PDF. Storage keeps your chosen settings on your device. An offscreen extension document assembles the PDF locally, without opening another visible window. webTopdf does not request access to every website in the background.

What we collect

Through the extension, nothing. The extension has no analytics, tracking pixels, account system, or capture-upload endpoint, and it runs no remote code. The extension does not keep a history of the pages you export.

The companion website

This website links to the Chrome Web Store and serves installation help and an optional developer download. It does not accept webpage URLs or upload page content. It does not use third-party fonts or advertising cookies. Since November 26, 2026 it counts page visits with Vercel Web Analytics, run by our host Vercel: the page address, the page you came from, the browser type and the country, with no cookies and no profile of you. The extension itself has no analytics and sends nothing (see What we collect).

The website’s hosting provider receives ordinary technical request information when you load a page, such as your IP address and browser headers. Hosting logs are governed by the provider’s practices. External links to the Chrome Web Store, GitHub, and Mulham Kassab’s portfolio are separate websites with their own policies.

Children

webTopdf is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children. The developer does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children, because no personal data is collected or sent off your device.

Your choices and data requests

You can clear saved extension settings by removing the extension in Chrome’s extension settings, and you can delete any PDFs you saved like any other file. Because the developer holds no personal data about you, there is normally nothing to access, correct or delete on a server. If you still want to ask whether the developer has any data about you, or want to make any other privacy request, write to the developer email shown on the Chrome Web Store listing and describe what you need. Installing and using webTopdf is optional.
