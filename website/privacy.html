PagePress turns webpages into PDFs on your device. It does not send page content, screenshots, or exported files to a PagePress server.

Effective date: October 12, 2026 · Applies to the PagePress extension and companion website.

The short version: no account, no capture upload, no analytics, and no advertising. You choose what to capture and where to save the resulting PDF.

What the extension handles

When you start a capture, PagePress temporarily reads information from the active tab, such as its title, URL, dimensions, and rendered appearance. A full-page export may scroll that page while taking screenshots. Export options and any layout preparation are handled in your browser.

PagePress combines those screenshots into a PDF locally and asks Chrome to download it. The PDF may contain the page’s title, URL, and visible content, including personal information shown on the page you chose. You control that file and any sharing you do afterward.

Permissions, with a purpose

The extension uses temporary access to your active tab when you interact with it. Scripting is used to inspect page dimensions, prepare the layout, and scroll during capture. Chrome’s download permission lets PagePress save the generated PDF. Storage keeps your chosen settings on your device. An offscreen extension document assembles the PDF locally, without opening another visible window. The latest export status is held temporarily for progress and download recovery; it is not a browsing-history log. PagePress does not request access to every website in the background.

What we collect

PagePress has no analytics, tracking pixels, account system, or capture-upload endpoint. The extension does not keep a history of the pages you export. Saved preferences are limited to the settings needed for the product.

The companion website

This website serves static files and runs its sample PDF demo locally. It does not use third-party fonts, analytics scripts, or advertising cookies. The sample content is fictional and is not connected to your open browser tabs.

The website’s hosting provider receives ordinary technical request information when you load a page, such as your IP address and browser headers. Hosting logs are governed by the provider’s practices. External links to GitHub and Mulham Kassab’s portfolio are separate websites with their own policies.

Your choices

You can clear saved extension preferences by removing the extension. PDFs already downloaded remain on your device until you delete them. Installing and using PagePress is optional, and you can remove it through Chrome’s extension settings at any time.

Changes and questions

This policy will be updated if the product’s data handling changes. The date above identifies the current version. For product questions or to report an issue, use the PagePress issue tracker. Please avoid including sensitive page content in public issues.
